1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

OmniWave 3D is engineered by Lalith Madanayake as a strictly 100% offline-native physics instrument and sensory laboratory. We believe in absolute user sovereignty and total digital privacy.

✔ Zero Telemetry: We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or analyze any personal data, usage metrics, location points, or device identifiers. The application operates entirely disconnected from any external servers or cloud services.

2. On-Device Sensor Permissions & Processing

To provide real-time scientific oscilloscopes and acoustic echolocation, OmniWave 3D requires access to specific device hardware sensors. All computations take place entirely in volatile, temporary RAM and are discarded immediately:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for active ultrasonic acoustic echolocation (19.2 kHz acoustic pings) and Doppler air-gesture velocity calculations. No ambient audio or voice is ever recorded, stored to local files, or broadcast over the network. If microphone permission is declined, the application seamlessly activates an internal acoustic physics simulation model so that radar visuals remain fully operational.
  • Accelerometer & Motion Sensors: High-speed 400 Hz MEMS kinetic sampling utilized solely to run the Butterworth gravity-isolation filter, detect tabletop surface micro-tremors, analyze machinery RPM vibration harmonics, and drive the "Stealth Table" game loop. Sensor readings are never persisted to disk.
  • Ambient Light Sensor: Utilized exclusively to compute optical ripple modulation, identify 100Hz/120Hz AC mains ballast flicker, and estimate circadian Kelvin color temperatures.
  • Haptic Motor & Vibrator (VIBRATE): Used purely to generate mechanical gear ratchet ticks, heavy rotary detents, and sonar echo impulses.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Traffic

OmniWave 3D guarantees a clean, unmonitored environment:

  • Zero Advertisements: No third-party ad networks, banners, or interstitials.
  • Zero Tracking SDKs: No Google Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or Crashlytics tracking.
  • Zero Cloud Infrastructure: The Android Manifest intentionally omits the android.permission.INTERNET permission, guaranteeing that the app cannot communicate with external servers even if attempted.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because OmniWave 3D does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, it is fully safe for audiences of all ages, including children under the age of 13. 5. Local Storage & App Preferences

The application only saves user-configurable preferences locally on your physical device using Android's standard SharedPreferences:

  • Sound toggle (Mute / Unmute)
  • Haptic feedback toggle (Enable / Disable)
  • Stealth Table party game personal best survival duration
  • Selected CRT phosphor color theme

This data never leaves your device and is permanently purged when the application is uninstalled.
