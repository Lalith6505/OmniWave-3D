ACOUSTICS

📡 EchoSonar 3D

Active acoustic echolocation utilizing near-ultrasonic sound waves. Directional bursts bounce off physical obstacles in pitch-black environments.

  • Submarine CRT Radar: 45 RPM rotating beam with calibrated distance rings (25cm, 50cm, 75cm, 100cm).
  • Acoustic Doppler Shift: Recognizes rapid hand gestures hovering above the phone screen.
  • Darkness Navigation: Maps physical proximity without using light or cameras.
KINETICS

⚡ Seismo-Core

High-frequency 400 Hz MEMS kinetic seismograph detecting subtle surface tremors, machinery vibrations, and footsteps.

  • Butterworth Gravity-Isolation: Strips standard 9.8 m/s² gravity vector to isolate pure micro-tremors.
  • Machinery Diagnostic Stethoscope: Place phone on motors or 3D printers to estimate peak RPM and harmonic stability.
  • The "Stealth Table" Party Game: Place phone on a table—anyone who thumps or walks too heavily triggers the klaxon alarm!
OPTICS

💡 Photon-Strobe

Optical oscilloscope measuring invisible high-frequency light modulation, LED driver ripple, and circadian eye ergonomics.

  • Strobe Hazard Detection: Exposes 100Hz/120Hz ballast flicker that causes ocular fatigue and migraines.
  • Circadian Kelvin Analysis: Estimates lighting color temperature (2200K warm candle to 6500K daylight).
  • Bulb Benchmark Presets: Compare clean DC illumination vs substandard AC mains ballasts.

🛡️ Privacy Policy & Security Guarantee

Read Full Privacy Document →

OmniWave 3D is designed with absolute privacy principles. All sensor computations (ultrasonic reflections, accelerometer kinematics, and optical flicker) execute exclusively in volatile RAM and are instantly discarded.

MICROPHONE PERMISSION Used strictly for acoustic time-of-flight echolocation. Zero audio is ever recorded, stored to local disk, or transmitted over any network.
ZERO EXTERNAL SERVERS The Android Manifest intentionally excludes internet permissions. The application cannot communicate with external servers even if prompted.
CHILD-SAFE & COPPA Because no personal identifiers or telemetry are ever collected, OmniWave 3D complies with global COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play family standards.
